FIRST INFORMATION REPORT

FIR No. 001/2024
Police Station: Andheri
District: Mumbai Suburban
Date of Report: 14/03/2024

IPC Section 394 and IPC Section 120B applied.

STATEMENT OF FACTS:

On 14 March 2024 at approximately 02:30 hours, a robbery was reported at the
Sunrise Jewellers showroom located in Andheri East, Mumbai. The complainant,
Suresh Patel, stated that four masked men entered the premises and took away
gold ornaments valued at Rs. 4,50,000.

Investigation identified the primary accused as Ravi Kumar, resident of
Andheri, who was previously arrested in a similar case. His mobile number
9876543210 was traced to the vicinity of the crime scene at the time of the
offence.

CCTV footage recorded a white Maruti Swift bearing registration number
MH01AB1234 leaving the scene. The vehicle is registered to Vikas Sharma,
a known associate of Ravi Kumar. Vikas Sharma uses mobile number 9823456789.

Call detail records show that Ravi Kumar was in contact with Mohammad Ali
of Delhi, phone number 9765432108, on multiple occasions before the robbery.
Mohammad Ali is suspected of coordinating the disposal of stolen goods
through a firm named Crescent Traders.

A payment of Rs. 2,50,000 was traced to bank account 918273645510 held in
the name of Anita Roy, who is believed to handle finances for the group.
Her PAN is ABCDE1234F.

The accused persons are believed to be part of an organised gang operating
across Mumbai and Delhi.
