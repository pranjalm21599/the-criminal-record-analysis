STATEMENT OF FACTS:

On 20 March 2024, a case of organised fraud and money laundering was
registered following a complaint by Deepak Malhotra, proprietor of a
trading firm in Karol Bagh, New Delhi.

Investigation revealed that the accused Mohammad Ali, operating from Delhi
using mobile number 9765432108, coordinated a network that routed proceeds
of crime through a series of accounts. Mohammad Ali is the same person named
in FIR 001/2024 registered at Andheri Police Station, Mumbai.

The accused Farhan Qureshi, resident of Karol Bagh, was found to be
operating mobile number 9911223344 and receiving instructions regarding
collection of cash. Farhan Qureshi holds bank account 776655443322.

Another accused, Imran Sheikh, using mobile number 9988776655, is suspected
of managing couriers. Imran Sheikh operates bank account 665544332211.

The accused Rakesh Gupta, phone number 9900112233, is believed to have
arranged shell companies under the name Meridian Exports for the purpose of
layering funds.

Funds amounting to Rs. 32,00,000 were traced through accounts held by the
accused persons. Anita Roy of Mumbai is suspected of coordinating the
transfers from the Mumbai end.
